# Gravity Spy glitch classes (what volunteers choose from)

Use these names when saying what a source may look like. Say "may appear as" unless the log itself names the class.

- **Blip:** a very short, symmetric "teardrop" burst across roughly 30-500 Hz. Its cause is usually unknown.
- **Repeating Blips:** blips that recur at a regular interval.
- **Koi Fish:** like a large, loud blip, wider at the base, with a fish-like outline.
- **Extremely Loud:** a very high-energy glitch that fills much of the plot, typical of large disturbances.
- **Scattered Light:** arches or stacked humps below about 100 Hz lasting seconds. Caused by stray light reflecting off moving surfaces, and more common when ground motion is high (microseism, wind, earthquakes, human activity).
- **Low Frequency Burst:** a short burst at the bottom of the plot, below about 50 Hz.
- **Low Frequency Line:** a steady horizontal line at low frequency.
- **Power Line (60 Hz):** a steady line at 60 Hz, the US mains frequency.
- **1080 Line:** a steady line near 1080 Hz.
- **Violin Mode Harmonic:** a line near 500 Hz or its multiples (about 1000, 1500 Hz), from vibrating mirror-suspension fibers. Often strong after locklosses until damped.
- **Whistle:** a narrow V or W shape, usually at high frequency, from radio-frequency beat notes.
- **Chirp:** a track rising in frequency over time, like a merging binary. Real candidates are rare; hardware injections look like this.
- **Scratchy:** a long band of rough, wavy noise at middle frequencies.
- **No Glitch / None of the Above:** for clean data or shapes not in the list.
